EPM vs TYNDP Gaps

Reference run S5_Base_v4 against TYNDP 2026 National Trends+ (draft), TYNDP 2024 National Trends and the national plans. Every gap on this page reads EPM minus the reference: negative means EPM is below.

What stands out

Romania solar, 2040
8.3 vs 21.4 GW

EPM stays at the committed fleet, while NT+ 2026 and the NECP both reach 21.4 GW. Batteries are 0.5 against 3.0 GW, and the whole fleet 34.8 against 53.1 GW.

Romania to Hungary, 2040
1,000 vs 3,700 MW

EPM limits stay flat because they come from 2025 flows, not from NTC values. 13 of 20 directions sit below NT+ 2026 in 2040, and 9 of 20 already sit below the 2025 NTC. Romania to Serbia is 800 against 2,650 MW.

Net exports, 2040
RO 5.2 vs 19.5 TWh

Bulgaria is the reverse, with 15.9 TWh in EPM against 7.7 in NT+ 2026. Together they export 21.1 against 27.1 TWh, so the total is closer than the split.

Demand, 2040
BG 39.4 vs 54.9 TWh

NT+ 2026 adds 12.4 TWh of electrolysers in Bulgaria. Romania is 73.4 against 84.2 TWh. Türkiye goes the other way, 645 against 582 TWh.

Bulgaria batteries, 2040
5.4 vs 1.1 GW

EPM already holds 5.4 GW in 2030. Türkiye is the other large gap above NT+ 2026, with 264 against 228 GW in 2040. Wind and gas are each about 18 GW higher, and solar is 11 GW lower.

How to read this

  • TYNDP 2026 is a draft. NT+ 2026 and NT 2024 are two separate scenario rounds and are never mixed here.
  • EPM has no 2035 on the five year grid. Capacity, net imports and limits show 2036 against 2035. Demand comes from the input series, which holds 2035.
  • EPM external limits are the 99th percentile of 2025 flows, not NTC. The Moldova and Ukraine limits follow annual volumes over 8,760 hours.
  • The NT 2024 grid is a 2030 grid. Its 2040 markers are the maximum hourly flow of climate year 2009, not a limit.
  • Georgia and Azerbaijan in TYNDP are PEMMDB inputs with no market run. TYNDP 2026 has no demand or border for them, and Armenia is in no edition.
  • Türkiye NT+ 2026 includes 5 GW of offshore wind that exists in PEMMDB only, and 5.5 GW of adequacy units under Other in 2035. Moldova solar is PEMMDB only as well.
  • NT+ 2026 demand leaves out pumping and battery load, so it is smaller than generation plus net imports.
  • Plan totals cover only the lines each plan gives. The Bulgarian plan has no storage line, and no plan is available for Azerbaijan.
  • Transelectrica values are export NTC for 2030 and 2033, shown at 2030 and 2035. Prices are not compared on this page.
